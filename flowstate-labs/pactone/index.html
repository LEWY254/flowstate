About Pactone

What is Pactone?

Pactone helps you follow through on habits and commitments you care about. Instead of tracking tasks in isolation, you create a goal (a pact), agree on how often you will report progress, and submit proof — a photo, screenshot, or other evidence — when each check-in is due. A friend can review your reports, or you can use solo mode and have Pactone verify them for you.

Who it’s for

Anyone building a routine who wants gentle, human accountability — not gamified streak pressure. Use it with a roommate, coach, or friend, or run goals privately on your own.

Morning workouts Studying Screen limits Daily writing Meditation

What you can do

  • Create goals with a schedule and expected proof type
  • Invite an accountability partner by @username
  • Submit check-in reports with photos or media
  • Track streaks, reliability, and upcoming due dates
  • Optionally attach a money stake held until the goal ends

How accountability works

When a report is due, you capture proof in the app. Your partner reviews it and can approve it or ask for a clearer submission. In solo mode, Pactone reviews your proof against what the goal requires. Missed check-ins are recorded honestly; optional stakes can transfer to your partner, charity, or back to you when the goal completes. Pactone does not sell your data or use engagement tricks — it is a calm tool to help you keep promises to yourself and people who matter.
